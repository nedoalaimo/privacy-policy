Privacy Policy for My Stuff

Last Updated: 04/14/2025

Thank you for using My Stuff ("App"). This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use this application.

Information We Collect

Currently, during the internal testing phase, the App aims to collect minimal information necessary for its core functionality and improvement:

  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous data about how you interact with the App (e.g., features used, button clicks) solely for the purpose of identifying bugs and improving the App's performance and user experience. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic, non-identifiable information about your device (e.g., device model, operating system version) to help diagnose technical issues.
  • Data You Provide: If the App allows you to input data (e.g., names or descriptions of tracked objects), this data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us unless explicitly stated for a specific feature (which is not currently implemented).

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts during this testing phase.

How We Use Information

The limited information collected is used solely for:

  • Providing and maintaining the App's functionality.
  • Identifying and fixing bugs or crashes.
  • Improving the App's features and performance based on anonymous usage patterns.
  • Ensuring compatibility with different devices.

Information Sharing

We do not share any collected information with third parties, except where required by law. Anonymous, aggregated usage data might be analyzed internally for development purposes but is never shared in a way that could identify an individual user.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the App. Data you input is stored locally on your device. However, please be aware that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or through other appropriate means. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
